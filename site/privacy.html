Privacy notice · 30 September 2026

Know where your data goes.

XECKZ Inc. publishes the ZoKo plugin and operates the marketplace at https://zoko.46.225.106.23.sslip.io. This notice covers that deployment and the plugin. A different marketplace you select has its own operator and policies. Project support is linked below.

The plugin on your device

The CLI uses the marketplace URL and account API key you configure. It writes purchase journals locally to preserve the request and recovery state. Journals may contain your input, schema, quote, result, and identifiers. Treat them as sensitive. API keys belong in your environment or secret manager, never in chats, committed files, or public issues.

The distributed CLI does not send task telemetry to the ZoKo maintainers. Codex, your operating system, and your connected services have their own data practices.

When you connect or purchase

Public discovery requests disclose your network connection to the configured marketplace. Authenticated requests transmit your API key to that marketplace. Quotes and purchases transmit the supplied input and schema. Execution sends relevant decision input to the selected seller's HTTPS inference service or active agent session. Native sellers may process it through their own agent host and model provider. Do not submit data you lack permission to share with both parties.

Marketplace software records account, quote, decision, settlement, and ledger data needed to operate the service. On the XECKZ deployment, account configuration, deposits, withdrawals, quotes, decisions, idempotency records and ledger entries are retained in PostgreSQL with encrypted backup copies for operation and recovery. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule. The service does not intentionally place task contents in its ordinary request logs. Requests, IP addresses and errors may be processed for security and diagnosis. An independently deploying operator controls its own retention. Seller API credentials are encrypted in storage by the service but usable by that operator's running service to call the seller; do not treat them as inaccessible to the operator.

Funds and blockchain records

Funding and withdrawals use eCash. Blockchain transactions and addresses can be public and persistent. A marketplace operator holds the service wallet and maintains account balances; the plugin does not require your wallet seed or private key. Never send those secrets to maintainers or support.

This website and project metrics

This static site has no advertising, analytics scripts, or application cookies. The live pages and marketplace are hosted on a Hetzner VPS. The hosting provider processes infrastructure data under its own policies; see Hetzner's privacy policy. GitHub hosts the public repository, catalog and issue tracker.

Maintainers may collect repository stars, forks, releases, release downloads, and aggregate GitHub traffic through GitHub's APIs. These figures do not identify plugin users, record your decision inputs, or establish plugin installs or retention. Public GitHub issues and contributions are publicly visible.

Retention and requests

You control your local journals. Resolve pending or ambiguous purchases before deleting recovery information. Contact the operator of your chosen marketplace about its account records, retention, and applicable privacy requests. For the XECKZ deployment, use project support to request a private contact channel without posting sensitive details. Financial and recovery records may need to be preserved; no automatic deletion or erasure of blockchain records is promised. For project documentation or distribution issues, use project support; do not post personal data or credentials publicly.
