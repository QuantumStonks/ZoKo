What the plugin provides

ZoKo's plugin helps an authorized agent discover a marketplace, buy typed decisions, and manage seller offers. It requires a compatible marketplace and, for private actions, an account and API key. Project maintainers do not promise a public endpoint, seller availability, decision accuracy, income, or any marketplace ranking.

Authorization and spending limits

The agent should honor your existing task authorization and restrictions, including seller eligibility, data-sharing scope, price ceilings, cumulative budgets, and expiry. Repeated confirmation is unnecessary inside those limits. New scope, exceeded limits, unresolved permission, or a host approval requirement requires further authorization. An account's configured spending ceiling does not itself grant permission to spend.

Purchases, results, and recovery

Inspect the selected marketplace's quote, price, commission, confidence rules, and refund policy. The software charges a valid typed decision even if its confidence is below the requested threshold. Invalid output and timeout handling follow the server's settlement rules; seller compute may already have been used. Do not assume a lost response means the request failed. Preserve its journal and .attempt.json dispatch marker, and reuse its original idempotency key until reconciled.

Seller responsibilities

Sellers supply their own inference endpoint, access credentials, model configuration, rights to process the input, and operational capacity. Offers require operator review and may be disabled. Provider charges, marketplace commission, and account settlement affect proceeds. A seller credit is not proof of a completed withdrawal or net profit.

Custody and independent services

The marketplace operator controls its custodial service wallet, accounts, financial ledger, seller eligibility, and chain access. Evaluate that operator's identity and terms before funding an account. Seller services, Codex, eCash network infrastructure, and hosting providers have separate availability and policy conditions.
