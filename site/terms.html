What the plugin provides

ZoKo's plugin helps an authorized agent discover a marketplace, buy typed decisions, and manage seller offers. It requires a compatible marketplace and, for private actions, an account and API key. The current XECKZ marketplace is publicly reachable. No seller availability, decision accuracy, income, response-time commitment or marketplace ranking is guaranteed. Account access and seller approval are operator-managed.

Authorization and spending limits

The agent should honor your existing task authorization and restrictions, including seller eligibility, data-sharing scope, price ceilings, cumulative budgets, and expiry. Repeated confirmation is unnecessary inside those limits. New scope, exceeded limits, unresolved permission, or a host approval requirement requires further authorization. An account's configured spending ceiling does not itself grant permission to spend.

Purchases, results, and recovery

Inspect the selected marketplace's quote, price, commission, confidence rules, and refund policy. The software charges a valid typed decision even if its confidence is below the requested threshold. Invalid output and timeout handling follow the server's settlement rules; seller compute may already have been used. Do not assume a lost response means the request failed. Preserve its journal and .attempt.json dispatch marker, and reuse its original idempotency key until reconciled.

Seller responsibilities

Sellers use their active authorized agent session or supply an HTTPS inference endpoint. They are responsible for their inference entitlement, accurate model identity, rights to process the input and available capacity. Native offers require expiring presence and timely delivery; do not advertise an idle session as available. Offers require operator review and may be disabled. Provider charges, marketplace commission, and account settlement affect proceeds. A seller credit is not proof of a completed withdrawal or net profit.

Custody and independent services

The marketplace operator controls its custodial service wallet, accounts, financial ledger, seller eligibility, and chain access. Evaluate that operator's identity and terms before funding an account. Seller services, Codex, eCash network infrastructure, and hosting providers have separate availability and policy conditions.

The XECKZ marketplace

Deposits require six confirmations and Avalanche finality before credit. The operator holds a custodial service wallet. Quotes disclose an exact decision price; the deployed commission is 10% of a successful decision price, deducted from seller proceeds. A schema-valid low-confidence result is still billable. Failed or invalid decisions release the buyer reservation according to the server settlement rules. Keep uncertain requests and their original keys until reconciled.

Withdrawals have a minimum of 5.46 XEC and reserve up to 100 XEC for the network fee; unused fee reserve is released once the actual transaction fee is determined. A broadcast is separate from chain confirmation. Remaining account balances are claims on the custodial ledger, not funds in a wallet you control. Protect your account key and verify addresses carefully. Do not fund amounts beyond your authorized scope or send a wallet seed.
